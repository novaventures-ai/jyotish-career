# Jyotish Career - The AI Cosmic Career Counselor 🔮✨

**Unlock your true professional destiny through the lens of Vedic Astrology.**

Jyotish Career is a comprehensive **Career Intelligence System** that fuses ancient Vedic algorithms (Parasara & Jaimini) with cutting-edge AI. We decode your birth chart to answer the most critical question: *"What was I born to do?"*

## 🌟 Key Features

### 🚀 Cosmic Career Pathfinder
Stop guessing. We analyze your **D1 (Birth)** and **D10 (Career)** charts to generate a precision-scored list of professions.
*   **Suitability Score**: See exactly how well you match with various career paths.
*   **Detailed Analysis**: Deep dive into the planetary influences driving your professional life.

### 🤖 AI Cosmic Counselor (Chat)
Your personal 24/7 astrologer.
*   **Context-Aware**: The AI knows your chart details (Planets, Houses, Dasha).
*   **Interactive Guidance**: Ask questions like *"Is this a good time to switch jobs?"* or *"How can I improve my relationship with my boss?"*

### 💰 Wealth & Earning Analysis
Decode your financial DNA.
*   **Wealth Yogas**: Discover hidden combinations for prosperity (Dhana Yogas) in your chart.
*   **Earning Sources**: Analyze your 2nd and 11th houses to determine your best sources of income (Active vs. Passive).

### 🛡️ Vedic SWOT Analysis
Know yourself to win the game.
*   **Strengths & Weaknesses**: A candid analysis of your inherent talents and potential blind spots based on planetary dignities.

### 📅 Dasha Timing System
Timing is everything.
*   **Vimshottari Dasha**: Visualize your current planetary period and how it influences your career focus.
*   **Timeline View**: See past and future periods to plan your career moves.

### ✅ AI Career Validator
Validate your choices.
*   **Role Check**: Input a specific job title or role, and the AI will validate if it aligns with your astrological signature.

### ✨ Remedies
Actionable, secular remedies to strengthen weak planets and boost your career luck.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
*   **Frontend**: React, Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
*   **Backend**: Node.js, Express
*   **Database**: Supabase (PostgreSQL)
*   **AI**: Google Gemini
*   **Deployment**: Vercel

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites
*   Node.js (v20+)
*   pnpm

### Installation
1.  Clone the repository:
    ```bash
    git clone https://github.com/novaventures-ai/jyotish-career.git
    ```
2.  Install dependencies:
    ```bash
    pnpm install
    ```
3.  Start the development server:
    ```bash
    npm run dev
    ```
